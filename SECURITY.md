# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please **do not** report vulnerabilities through public Issues, Discussions, or pull requests.

Report privately by email to **info@masteritsupport.com** with:
- the affected product and version,
- a description of the issue and its impact,
- whether it is already public or has a CVE.

Do not include credentials, customer data, or your network's IP addresses unless we ask for them through a secure channel.

## What to expect
- We acknowledge receipt within 3 business days.
- We coordinate with the affected vendor before publishing an advisory.
- With your permission, we credit you by the public name you choose.

## Scope
This repository publishes advisories based on official vendor and NVD data. Errors in an advisory (wrong version, broken link) can be reported through a public Issue, without any network details.
